# Your portfolio: how to use the dashboard

Hi Najeeb! This guide explains how to update your website yourself. You never need to touch code.

---

## 1. Logging in

1. Go to **yourdomain.com/admin** (until you have a domain, use the address Abid gives you, ending in `/admin`).
2. Enter your email and password, then press **Log in**.
3. You stay logged in for 7 days on that device.

**Forgot your password?** There's no "forgot password" link, on purpose (it keeps the site safer). Ask Abid to reset it for you. It takes one minute.

**Wrong password 5 times?** The login is locked for 15 minutes. Just wait and try again.

---

## 2. First-time setup

Open **Overview** (the first page after logging in). The **Setup checklist** shows what's still missing. Click any line to jump straight to the right place. Good things to do first:

- Upload a **profile photo** (Profile → Basics).
- Check your **resume** is the latest one (Profile → Basics → Resume).
- For each game: upload a **cover image** and **screenshots**, and add the **itch.io / Play Store link** if there is one.
- Replace the two game descriptions marked in the checklist with your own words.
- Check your **skill levels** (Skills): the percentages are estimates, change them to what feels right, or switch percentages off.
- **Change your password** (Account).

The checklist disappears when everything is done.

---

## 3. Adding a new game

1. Click **Games** in the left menu, then **+ Add game**.
2. Fill in the **Title** (the page address is created for you), a **Short description** (1–2 sentences for the card) and the **Genre** (this makes the filter buttons on your site).
3. Optional but recommended:
   - **Full description**: story, features, controls. Use the toolbar for **bold**, headings and lists. Click **Preview** to see how it looks.
   - **Cover image**: drag a picture onto the box or click **Upload**. If you don't have one, pick one of the 4 generated pictures; they change colour with your theme.
   - **Screenshots**: click **Add screenshots** and select several images at once. Drag them to change the order.
   - **Trailer**: paste a YouTube link.
   - **Play / store links**: click **+ Add link**, choose the store (itch.io, Google Play, …) and paste the link.
4. Switch **Published** on when it's ready (off = draft, only you can see it).
5. Click **Create game**. Done: it's on your site within seconds.

**Changing the order of games:** in the Games list, drag a game by the ⋮⋮ handle. The order on your site follows instantly.

**Featured:** click the ☆ star in the list (or the switch in the game form) to add a "★ Featured" badge.

---

## 4. Changing the theme (colours, fonts, effects)

1. Click **Theme**.
2. Click one of the 9 ready-made themes. The preview on the right changes immediately.
3. Fine-tune if you like:
   - **Custom colours**: click a colour square to pick a new colour.
   - **Contrast check**: if a line shows ⚠ Low, text may be hard to read. Try a lighter text or darker background.
   - **Typography**: choose a font style.
   - **Corner roundness**, **Background effect** (Starfield / Grid / None) and **Animations**.
4. Visitors don't see anything yet. When you're happy, click **Publish theme**. Click **Discard** to throw the changes away.

Tip: **Save as my theme…** keeps your custom colours so you can switch back to them later.

---

## 5. Showing, hiding and reordering sections

1. Click **Layout**.
2. **Drag** sections to reorder them. **Switch off** a section to hide it (its menu link disappears too).
3. Type in the label box to rename the menu link (for example "Games" → "Projects").
4. Under **Headings & text**, open a section to change its titles. The last two words of each title get the colourful gradient.
5. Click **Save changes** at the bottom (or press Ctrl+S / Cmd+S).

The **Testimonials** section stays hidden until you publish at least one testimonial. Only add real quotes from real people, with their permission.

---

## 6. Reading and replying to messages

1. Click **Messages** (a number shows how many are new).
2. Click a message to read it. It's then marked as read.
3. Click **Reply** to answer from your email app.
4. **Archive** keeps a message but moves it out of the way; **Delete** removes it forever.
5. **Export CSV** downloads your messages as a spreadsheet.

A ⚠ next to a message means the email notification didn't arrive in your inbox, but the message itself is safe here.

---

## 7. Your resume (CV)

Profile → Basics → **Resume**. Click **Replace** and choose the new PDF (max 5 MB). The old resume is **deleted automatically**, so there's only ever one, and the **Download CV** button on your site always gives the newest one. This saves immediately.

---

## 8. Image tips

| Image | Best size |
|---|---|
| Game cover | 1600 × 1000 (wide) |
| Screenshots | any, landscape looks best |
| Profile photo | 800 × 800 or larger (square) |
| Social share image (SEO) | 1200 × 630 |
| Favicon | square PNG, 512 × 512 |

- Images can be up to **8 MB** (JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF or AVIF).
- **Always fill in the alt text** (a short description of the picture). It helps blind visitors and Google.
- All your files are in **Media**. A file that's in use can't be deleted; the page tells you where it's used.

---

## 9. Backups (once a month)

Click **Backup** → **Download backup (.json)** and keep the file somewhere safe (Google Drive, email it to yourself…).
If something goes badly wrong, Abid (or you) can restore it from the same page.

---

## 10. Costs and limits

Everything runs on free plans. Limits to keep an eye on:

- **ImageKit** (images and resume): the free plan includes plenty of storage and bandwidth for a portfolio. The Media page shows how much you use.
- **Resend** (contact-form emails): 100 emails per day.
- **MongoDB Atlas** (your content): 512 MB, far more than a portfolio needs.
- **Vercel** (hosting): free hobby plan.
